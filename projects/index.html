An Analysis of Small Modular Reactors as an Operational Response to Net-Zero Energy Demand in Ontario

Electricity consumption in Canada is expected to grow beyond its current generational capacity in the following decades. Canada's commitment to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 constrains the set of feasible solutions greatly. In this report, small modular reactors (SMR) are explored as an operational necessity to realize Canada's growing energy demand while achieving net-zero. Small modualr reactors are a very novel technology that have yet to be used for commercial generation in Western nations; Ontario is on track to construct and operate the G7's first SMR in Darlington.

AffectFusion: A Multimodal Deep Learning Framework for Affective State Recognition with Applications to Online Education Systems

Educational Technology is an area that may benefit from machine learning in a number of ways. One of these ways is live detection of student engagement, understanding, and overall sentiment during a lesson. This is a challenge that is not unique to online learning; in fact online learning (if paired with a webcam and microphone) is an environment in which machine learning can be most effective. In this report we present AffectFusion, a framework that monitors emotion and engagement with a series of models that predict a student's affective state on their face and voice, outputting one unified prediction.

An Information-Theoretic Framework for Manufacturing Operations: the Error-Correcting Capacity of the Toyota Production System

Through the perspective of information and coding theory, I took a management-like course in Operations and Production Management and saw an interesting connection between the setup and goals of both error control coding and effective manufacturing management. I believe my mathematical background helped me see these processes more abstractly: both coding and manufacturing attempt to take an input, translate, transmit, output, and revert back to the original intended input, all while optimizing for energy, time, and errors.
